Every kind of car rental in Madurai, under one booking line

From a self-drive hatchback for the weekend to a full wedding fleet, here's what Drive With Prabha runs — and who each service tends to suit best.

On this page

Everyday rental

Self-drive, chauffeur-driven & 24x7 car rental

Self-Drive Car Rental

Hold the wheel yourself for errands, short trips or a weekend away. Ideal for confident local drivers. Book with a valid licence and ID at least a day ahead.

Chauffeur-Driven Car Rental

A trained driver handles the route while you relax. Best for guests, families with children, or anyone unfamiliar with Madurai's roads. Book with pickup point and time.

24x7 Car Rental in Madurai

Round-the-clock availability for last-minute trips, hospital runs or late flights. Suited to travellers who can't plan around business hours. Call or WhatsApp any time to book.

Weddings & events

Wedding, event & local sightseeing rental

Wedding Car Rental in Madurai

Decorated sedans and SUVs for the baraat, reception and guest transport. Best for families planning the full function schedule. Book at least two weeks ahead in peak season.

Event Transportation

Coordinated pickups for conferences, engagements and family functions. Suited to event planners managing multiple guests. Book with a full guest and timing list.

Local Sightseeing

Half-day and full-day circuits covering Meenakshi Amman Temple and nearby landmarks. Ideal for pilgrims and first-time visitors. Book the evening before for a morning start.

Airport, corporate & travel

Airport pickup, corporate & outstation travel

Car Rental in Madurai Airport

Flight-tracked pickup and drop at Madurai Airport. Best for business and holiday flyers. Share your flight number when booking so the driver adjusts to delays.

Corporate Travel

Scheduled sedans and SUVs for client visits and staff transport, billed with clean invoices. Suited to companies with recurring travel needs. Set up a standing monthly account.

Family Trip Packages

Multi-seat MPVs with luggage space for multi-day family holidays. Ideal for groups of five or more. Book with your full itinerary for an accurate quote.

Outstation Trips

One-way and round trips to nearby cities and hill stations. Best for travellers who don't want to drive long distances. Book two to three days ahead for outstation permits.

Hotel Pickup

Direct pickup from any Madurai hotel lobby. Suited to visiting guests without local transport. Share your hotel name and room booking reference.

Railway Station Pickup

Timed pickup at Madurai Junction, tracked against train arrival. Ideal for travellers with heavy luggage. Share your PNR so the driver can track delays.

Long-term & specialised

Monthly, luxury & customised rental

Luxury Car Rental in Madurai

Premium SUVs and sedans for special occasions and VIP guests. Best for clients who want comfort as much as reliability. Book with preferred model in advance.

Monthly & Long-Term Rental

A dedicated car and driver on a fixed monthly rate. Suited to professionals on long assignments in Madurai. Sign a simple monthly agreement to start.

VIP Transportation

Discreet, punctual transport for dignitaries and senior executives. Ideal for organisations hosting official visits. Book with full security and timing requirements.

Customised Travel Packages

A route and vehicle plan built around a specific trip that doesn't fit a standard package. Best for unusual itineraries. Describe your plan on the Contact page for a tailored quote.
