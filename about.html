Our story

From one car to a fleet Madurai relies on

Prabha began driving tourists and pilgrims around Madurai more than a decade ago, ferrying families to Meenakshi Amman Temple at sunrise and businesspeople to the airport late at night. The requests kept expanding — a wedding car for a cousin's function, a weekend SUV for a hill-station trip, a monthly rental for a visiting engineer — until a one-car operation grew into Drive With Prabha, a proper car rental company with a maintained fleet and a trained team of drivers.

What hasn't changed is the way bookings are handled: a real phone call or WhatsApp message, a clear quote, and a driver who actually knows the destination. That's still the difference between a taxi app and a car rental in Madurai you can plan a wedding or a business trip around.
