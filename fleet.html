How pricing works

Reading the price list

Daily rates include a standard kilometre allowance for in-city use; outstation and wedding bookings are quoted separately based on distance and duration. Hourly rates apply to short, in-city bookings such as airport pickup or a few hours of local sightseeing. Every quote — daily or hourly — includes the driver allowance, so the figure you receive on the Contact page is the final car rental in Madurai with driver price, before tolls and interstate permits where applicable.

Availability changes with wedding season and festival dates, so vehicles marked "Book ahead" are best reserved a week or two in advance. For same-day or overnight requests, our 24x7 car rental in Madurai line is the fastest way to confirm what's free right now.

A few practical notes before you book: self-drive rentals require a valid driving licence and a government photo ID, chauffeur-driven bookings only need your pickup point and time, and outstation trips may need an additional day's notice for permit paperwork. If you're weighing a wedding car rental in Madurai against a standard chauffeur booking, our team can walk you through which fleet combination suits your guest count and function timings — just mention it when you reach out on the Contact page.
